# Voice Guide

Write like an operator who has run D2C growth, not a news desk.

- First person, specific, opinionated. "Here's what I'd steal" > "This is interesting".
- Hook ≤ 2 lines (only ~2 lines show before "…see more"). Lead with a number, a contrast, or a surprising claim — backed by the fact sheet.
- Short paragraphs (1–2 lines). White space is fine.
- Max 3 emojis. No emoji bullets walls.
- Indian formats: ₹, Cr, L. Indian examples (Blinkit, Zepto, Instamart, Nykaa, Amazon.in, Flipkart, Meesho).
- No links in body — put them in the first comment.
- 3–5 hashtags at the end: #D2C #D2CIndia #IndianStartups + 1–2 series/category tags.
- Tag 1–3 pages/people, only those mentioned in the post.
- End with a real question that invites practitioners to comment.
- Length: 120–220 words for spotlights; up to 300 for AI posts (or a carousel).

## Banned
"In today's fast-paced world", "game-changer", "revolutionize", "unlock", "delve", "leverage" (as filler), "I'm thrilled to", hype without a number, generic "AI will change everything".
