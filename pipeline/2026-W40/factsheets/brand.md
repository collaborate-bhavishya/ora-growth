## One thing to steal
Own a named, proprietary active and make it the hero SKU; let a science-led story (not influencer spend) carry the brand — including into quick commerce.
Evidence: MelaMe™ branding + landing page + 14-day claim; BusinessLine quote on research over influencers; ₹100 Cr ARR run rate & 16x growth; Blinkit 17% of revenue; 35% on own site.
Caveat: the causal link (active → growth) is our interpretation — phrase as opinion.
