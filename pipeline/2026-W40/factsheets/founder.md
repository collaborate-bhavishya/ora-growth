## Quotes
- Sarthak Aggarwal: "We started Theater because we couldn't find beautiful, well-made accessories in India that weren't either a luxury import or a compromise. So we decided to build them ourselves - made in India and priced for a generation that cares deeply about design but doesn't want to pay luxury prices." — Entrepreneur India
- Arvind Kothari, Niveshaay: "…Footwear, stockings and bags remain largely unorganised, with limited design-led options for the aspirational Indian woman. Theater has built exactly that, with evergreen, design-led products that consumers have discovered organically."

## The insight
Pick a largely unorganised category, own "mass-premium" (between luxury import and unbranded compromise), prove demand online + quick commerce, then take growth capital offline.
