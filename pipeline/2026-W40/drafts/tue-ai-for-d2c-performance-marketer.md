## Post
Amazon's Great Indian Festival starts 8 Oct. Festive CPMs can spike up to 40%.
This is the worst time to run your ad account the 2023 way.

Here's a D2C performance marketer's week, before and after AI 👇

1. Monday reporting
Before: 3–4 hours of exports, pivots and a deck.
After: Google's Ads/Analytics Advisor plus an LLM on your CSVs draft the pivots, the anomalies and the story. You check the numbers.

2. Creative testing
Before: 4–6 new ads a week, limited by design bandwidth.
After: Advantage+ creative and Google's Asset Studio make the variants. Meta's Andromeda delivery system is widely read as rewarding creative diversity, so your job is picking concepts, not resizing banners.

3. Hook ideation
Before: a brainstorm.
After: feed 500 reviews and comments into an LLM, get pain-point clusters and 30 hooks, including Hinglish UGC scripts.

4. Regional creative
Before: separate shoots per language.
After: Meta AI's Reels translation (a creator tool) now dubs into Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada and Marathi.

5. Attribution
Before: GA4 last-click and platform self-reporting.
After: Google's open-source Meridian MMM, with an LLM helping you set it up.

What stays human:
→ Deciding what's true. One independent study of 250+ retail campaigns on Google's AI Max found median revenue up 13% but median CPA up 16%, while Google reports 14% more conversions at similar CPA/ROAS. Platforms grade their own homework.
→ Brand and claim approval. Never let auto-enhanced creative go live unreviewed.
→ Cash, inventory and festive pacing.

AI is taking over the execution. You still own the judgement.

Performance marketers: which of these have you already handed off?
