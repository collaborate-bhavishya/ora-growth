## Post
A ₹599 dark-spot serum.
Behind it, a skincare brand now at a ₹100 Cr ARR run rate.

This week's Brand Teardown: Asaya 🧪

The product:
The Advanced Spot Targeting Serum is built around MelaMe™, Asaya's own patent-pending complex for hyperpigmentation, paired with 5% niacinamide and 2% alpha arbutin.
It targets one specific problem: hyperpigmentation in melanin-rich Indian skin.

The positioning:
Science-led, not celebrity-led.
Asaya is betting that research, not influencer-led marketing, will be its biggest differentiator.

The growth engine:
Amazon and Nykaa are reportedly its largest online channels. About 35% of revenue reportedly comes from its own website, and Blinkit alone reportedly contributes 17%.
Next up: offline retail across Tier 1 and 2 cities, after an ₹88 Cr Series A.

One thing to steal 👇
Give your hero product a named ingredient you own.
"Niacinamide serum" is a commodity. "MelaMe™" is a story competitors can't easily copy. And my read is that a single-problem product is an easier sell when someone is scrolling a quick-commerce app.

Congrats to Eeti Sharma, Neeraj Biyani, Mandeep Singh Bhatia and team Asaya.

Beauty founders: is your hero SKU built on an ingredient everyone has, or one only you have?
