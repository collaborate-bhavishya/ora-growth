This Founder Friday: Sarthak Aggarwal, Vikram Jain, Karan Jain and Shruti Aggarwal, who just raised a ₹75 Cr Series A led by Niveshaay 🎉

The gap they spotted:
As the founders tell it, Indian shoppers had two options. A luxury import, or a compromise.
Theater built the middle: design-led, made in India, priced for people who care about design but won't pay luxury prices.

The playbook I'd steal:
1. Pick a messy category. When a space is largely unorganised, a brand with a clear point of view stands out.
2. Own "mass-premium". Heels at ₹2,400–3,490 aim for the space between unbranded options and luxury imports.
3. Prove it online first. D2C and marketplaces first, and the earlier round was aimed at quick commerce.
4. Then scale offline. This round is meant to accelerate offline expansion across Tier 1 and 2 cities.

Reported revenue grew 2.37X to ₹33.35 Cr in FY25, with a stated focus on profitability.

Congratulations to the Theater team 👏
